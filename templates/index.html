    Brain Tumor Pathology Atlas

    Key histological categories and imaging characteristics classified by the model

    🔴

    Glioma

    Originates from glial cells (astrocytoma, oligodendroglioma, glioblastoma multiforme). Frequently displays invasive infiltration into parenchymal tissue, central necrosis, and microvascular proliferation.

    Aggressiveness: Grade II–IV Dataset Slices: 1,426
    🟡

    Meningioma

    Develops from arachnoid cells within the meningeal layers surrounding brain and spinal cord. Typically well-circumscribed, extra-axial, showing robust homogeneous post-gadolinium enhancement and dural tail signs.

    Aggressiveness: Mostly Grade I Dataset Slices: 708
    🟣

    Pituitary Adenoma

    Sellar and suprasellar masses arising from anterior pituitary lobe epithelium. Can cause hormone dysregulation (hyperprolactinemia, Cushing's) and visual field compromise via optic chiasm compression.

    Aggressiveness: Benign Adenoma Dataset Slices: 930
